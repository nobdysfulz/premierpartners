Premier Partners 01 · Introduction
How to use this guide

A field guide to the brand.

This document is the working reference for anyone designing, writing, or producing material on behalf of Premier Partners.

The system is dark-first, period-stacked, and built around a single accent color. Hold the line on these decisions and the brand stays cohesive across every touchpoint.

When this guide conflicts with the original brand lookbook, defer to the lookbook.

Premier Partners 02 · Copywriting rules
Mechanics

The rules
that hold it together.

Punctuation

  • No em dashes. Use periods, commas, or line breaks.
  • Periods, not exclamation points, even on calls to action.
  • Oxford comma, always.
  • Curly quotes in body copy. Real apostrophes. Proper ellipses.

Casing

  • Headlines: ALL CAPS. Montserrat Bold or Black.
  • Body: sentence case. Open Sans Regular.
  • Eyebrows: ALL CAPS, tracked 0.18em.
  • The wordmark is always all caps, tracked 0.32em.

Voice

  • The team is "we." The reader is "you."
  • No first-person singular in marketing copy.
  • Numerals for prices, square footage, beds and baths.
  • Spell out numbers under ten in narrative.
  • No emoji. Not anywhere.

Premier Partners 07 · Photography · Direction
Photography direction

Rich blacks.
Cool midtones.

Do

  • Modern architecture lit from inside at dusk.
  • Silver, slate, and platinum tones. Crisp interior glow.
  • Slight contrast push. Lifted cool midtones.
  • People mid-action: walking, talking, signing.
  • Agent portraits in color, slightly desaturated.

Don't

  • Warm gold or amber casts.
  • HDR or heavy filters.
  • Flat lighting. Daylight stock photography.
  • Posed group shots against neutral walls.
  • Autoplay video heroes with sound.

Premier Partners 10 · Handoff
Quick reference

Ten things
to get right.

  • Dark surface first. White on jet by default.
  • Premier Blue is the only chromatic color. Use it like punctuation.
  • All headlines ALL CAPS. Montserrat Bold or Black.
  • Body copy in Open Sans Regular, sentence case.
  • No em dashes. Periods, commas, or line breaks.
  • No emoji. Anywhere.
  • "EVERYTHING." sets in Premier Blue inside the tagline.
  • Eyebrow → 88px accent rule → headline. Repeat the pattern.
